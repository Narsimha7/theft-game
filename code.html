Covert Protocol: Game Rules

3 Detectives (Civilian Operatives)

You receive the secret word (e.g., "Vault"). Give subtle clues that your teammates recognize, but don't make it too obvious for the Thief!

1 Undercover Agent

You receive a related word (e.g., "Keypad"). You don't know you're undercover until clues start sounding unusual. Blend in and deflect suspicion!

1 The Thief (Mr. White)

You receive NO word! Listen carefully to other clues, improvise a believable clue, and if voted out, you can still steal victory by correctly guessing the Detectives' word!
