Covert Protocol: How To Play

Detectives (Civilian Operatives)

You receive the shared confidential keyword (e.g., "Safe"). Give precise, subtle clues to prove you know the word without revealing it to the Thief!

Undercover Agent

You receive a slightly different word (e.g., "Keypad" or "Pincode"). You don't realize you have a different word until clues begin to deviate! Blend in and survive the vote.

The Thief (Mr. White)

You receive NO word at all! Listen carefully to other players' clues, improvise a plausible clue, and if voted out, you can still steal the win by guessing the Detectives' word!
